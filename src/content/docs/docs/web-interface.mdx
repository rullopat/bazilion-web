`bazilion dashboard` runs the web UI on `http://127.0.0.1:4322` and connects it to the daemon on
`127.0.0.1:4321`. The web UI and the CLI offer the same management features.

## Sign in and first-run setup

Sign in on `/login` with the bootstrap token (`bazilion token show-local` prints it) or a device
token. The daemon gives the browser a session cookie; the token itself is not stored. A session
ends after 30 days without use.

Until one provider is connected, every page leads to `/welcome`. Choose a provider, paste its
API key (or sign in with ChatGPT, or use a model server found on this computer) and press
**Connect and test**: nothing is saved unless a model answers. Bazilion then creates a `default`
Profile and a `default` Team, and **Start chatting** opens your first Agent
([getting started](/docs/getting-started/#sign-in-and-connect-a-provider)).

<Aside type="tip">
Create a token per browser or phone under **Setup → Access**. Revoking a token ends the browser
sessions opened with it. The bootstrap token cannot be revoked.
</Aside>

## The workspace (`/`)

The home screen is one workspace: your Teams and their Agents on the left, the open Agent's
conversation in the middle, and everything about the work on the right. Agents and Teams have no
pages of their own.

![The workspace: the list of Teams and Agents with a state each, Dev's conversation with its steps, and the Changes tab with a diff.](/screenshots/workspace.webp)

The top bar has **Jump to** (Ctrl+K, or ⌘K on a Mac), then three places: **Attention**, with the
number of open items, kept current on every page; **Manage**; and **Setup**. The person icon at the
end holds the theme (the same as the computer, light or dark), desktop notifications and **Log
out**. The browser tab names the page and counts what waits for you, as in `(1) Quill · Launch —
bazilion`.

### The overview

With nothing open, the middle of the workspace is an overview: who is waiting for you, who is
working, every Team with its members, the latest files from every Team, and the Agents that were
active last. A Team opened on its own shows its members with what each is doing, its views,
**Today** (its day at a glance: hand-offs and replies between its Agents, files delivered,
conversations worked in, questions and approvals waiting, newest first), its latest files and
what its Agents said to each other lately.

![The workspace with nothing open: an overview of who is waiting for you, who is working, the Teams and the latest files.](/screenshots/overview.webp)

### The list

Every Team is listed with its Agents, and every Agent has a line that says what it is doing:

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Waiting for you: a question` | The Agent asked a [question](/docs/questions/) and waits for your answer |
| `Waiting for you: a command to approve` | A flagged shell command waits for **Allow once** or **Deny** |
| `Working: writing theme.js` | A turn is running; the line follows its latest step |
| `Follow-ups paused` | Its [follow-up queue](/docs/follow-up-queue/) is paused with messages waiting |
| `2 new files`, `New activity` | Something happened since this browser last had its conversation open |
| `Idle`, `No conversation yet` | Nothing is running |

A Team's header sums its Agents up (`1 waiting · 2 working`). **New** spawns an Agent from a
Profile or a whole Team from a Team template, or registers a Team. The menu beside an Agent
renames or archives it. What counts as "new" is remembered in the browser, so another browser has
its own markers.

### The conversation

The middle pane streams the open Agent's conversation.

- Tool calls read as steps in words: `Ran`, `Read`, `Wrote` with the lines added and removed.
  Click a step for its input and output; a running step shows a live tail.
- A message to a teammate is a hand-off card with the teammate's name, the message and what that
  teammate is doing now.
- Delivered files show where they were delivered, pictures as pictures
  ([results](/docs/results/)).
- When a turn wrote files, **Review changes** under it opens their diff.
- A question from the Agent has one button per choice. While an Agent waits on you, its step and
  the line under the conversation say `waiting for you`, in amber, as the list does; green and
  `working` mean it is working.
- **Edit**, under your last message, appears when you point at it and replaces that turn; what
  you had typed comes back afterwards.
- Going to another Agent and back opens the conversation where you left it, with the text you
  had not sent still in the composer.
- Attach files with the button, by pasting or by dragging them in. Images reach the model as
  images; other files are saved for the Agent to read.
- While a turn runs, **Send** becomes **Queue follow-up** and **Stop** cancels the turn; the
  reply then ends with `Stopped.`
- With `BAZILION_BASH_APPROVAL=dangerous`, a flagged shell command shows **Allow once** and
  **Deny**.
- Composer commands: `/new` (conversations), `/compact` (summarise older history) and `/context`
  (what the next turn would carry).

The bar above the conversation names the Agent, its Team and its model. Its menu lists the Agent's
[conversations](/docs/conversations/): read an earlier one, continue it, start a new one or rename
one.

![Mira's conversation with a hand-off card for three teammates, and the Connections tab beside it.](/screenshots/handoffs.webp)

![A question from an Agent with one button per choice, and the Files tab with delivered pictures.](/screenshots/question.webp)

### The side panel

The panel has five tabs and remembers the last one; the button at the right of the conversation's
bar hides it.

- **Agent**: model and reasoning, saved as soon as you pick them, with **Undo**; the Team the
  Agent belongs to; rename, archive and delete; and the ways into its schedules, learning,
  mailbox, skills, Telegram topic and managed browser. The model is chosen by name, under its
  provider, with how much it reads and whether it sees images.
- **Team**: the members with their states (left out beside a Team's own overview), **About you**
  (`USER.md`), the shared memory with a search, and settings (the workspace folder and its
  commands in Docker, Telegram topic names, **Save as a Team template**), and the Team folder
  behind **Show the folder**. A new Team's `USER.md`
  starts empty: write it, or let its Agents fill it in as they learn about you.
- **Connections**: whether everyone can message everyone or only along connections, who may
  message whom, and the latest
  messages between its Agents ([Team Policy](/docs/concepts/#team-policy)).
- **Changes**: the files the Team's workspace changed since a commit, with the Agent that wrote
  each, and one file's diff ([reviewing what changed](/docs/coding-review/)). Only for a Team whose
  folder is a Git repository.
- **Files**: what the Team's Agents delivered, by kind (all, pictures, documents) and by whom
  (the whole Team, one member, the conversation on screen), with the same filters as the gallery.

On a phone the panel's tabs scroll sideways.

The deeper pages open as sheets over the workspace: an Agent's **Schedules**, **Learning**
([daily operations](/docs/operations/#reviewed-learning)), **Mailbox**, **Skills**, **Telegram**
and **Browser**; a Team's **About you**, memory and settings. They use the same cards and fields
as Setup and Manage. A schedule reads as words, such as `Weekdays at 09:00`, says when it runs
next, and is added in a dialog with presets, or as a cron expression. Memory notes are listed by title and read
formatted; **Edit** opens the text.

![Mira's schedules: a schedule that reads Weekdays at 09:00 with its message and a switch, and the recent runs below.](/screenshots/schedules.webp)

## Live, and notifications

Everything that shows what Agents are doing follows it as it happens: the list, the conversation,
the overview, the top bar's count, the tab title and the Attention page. The daemon pushes each
change over one event stream, so a question shows the moment it is asked. One tab per browser
holds the stream and shares it with the others. While it cannot connect, for example while the
daemon restarts, the pages ask every few seconds and try the stream again. A Team or an Agent
added, renamed, moved or archived somewhere else (in a terminal, by an Agent running
`bazilion`, in another tab) shows up without a reload, and a question asked or answered
elsewhere comes and goes in the open conversation.

**Notify me on this computer**, in the menu at the end of the top bar, turns on desktop
notifications for this browser, after the browser asks you. When an Agent starts waiting for you
and no Bazilion tab has the focus, a notification names the Agent and its question or command;
clicking it opens the conversation. Browsers offer notifications on `https://` addresses and on
this computer (`127.0.0.1`, `localhost`) only. If a browser blocks them for the site, the menu
says so. They come with the live updates: while those are down, none are shown.

When you are not at the computer, [Telegram](/docs/telegram/) reaches you: every question is
posted in the Agent's topic, and a command still waiting for approval after half a minute too.

## Jump to, and the keyboard

**Jump to** lists the Agents waiting for you first, then those working, then actions (a new
conversation with the open Agent, adding an Agent, switching the theme, the keyboard shortcuts),
the other Agents by when they were last active, recent conversations, Teams, the open Team's views
and the Manage and Setup pages. Type to narrow it down; Enter opens the one highlighted, also when
you press it before the lists have arrived.

![Jump to with Quill waiting for an answer at the top, Scout working, and actions to start a conversation, add an Agent, switch the theme and list the shortcuts.](/screenshots/palette.webp)

Three characters or more also search your conversations. **In conversations** lists the messages
holding every word typed, yours and the Agents' replies, with the words marked; opening one shows
its conversation at that message, marked for a moment. Tool output is not searched, and a stored
key never turns up. From a terminal: `bazilion conversation search "dark mode"`.

![Jump to with "dark mode" typed: messages from Pixel, Quill, Dev and Mira that hold both words, each with its Agent and conversation.](/screenshots/search.webp)

| Keys | |
| --- | --- |
| ⌘K or Ctrl+K | Jump to |
| `?` | The list of shortcuts |
| `/` | Write to the open Agent |
| ⌥↓ ⌥↑, or Alt+↓ Alt+↑ | The next or previous Agent in the list |
| ⌘. or Ctrl+. | Show or hide the side panel |

The keys without ⌘ or Ctrl wait while you type in a field or a list is open.

## Full-width views

Connections, Changes and Files also open across the whole width, with the list folded to avatars.
Switch between them at the top of the view; the arrow leads back to the conversation.

### Connections

The Team as a map. Every Agent is a node with what it is doing; every pair that is connected or
that talked is one line, with an arrowhead for each allowed direction and the number of recent
messages. Drag from one Agent to another to connect them. Select an Agent or a line to switch
each direction and to read what they said to each other. **Who can message whom** sets the
Team's mode, **Everyone** or **Connections only**; drawing a connection between teammates of an
open Team asks to switch it first. **Table** shows the same connections as a matrix, and nothing
changes until **Save**.

![The Connections map of a Team, with the line between two Agents selected and their messages beside it.](/screenshots/connections.webp)

### Changes

The changed files on the left, and the selected file's diff with the old and the new file side by
side (or in one column). A new or deleted file opens in one column, and a new Markdown file has a
**Preview**. Click a line number to comment on that line, or **Comment on the file**.
Comments gather into one review; **Send review** sends them to one Agent as a single message,
which it runs as its next turn.

![The Changes view: a side-by-side diff with two line comments and the review about to be sent.](/screenshots/changes.webp)

### Files

A gallery of everything the Team's Agents delivered. Filter by kind and by whom, as in the Files
tab, open a file for
its details and the conversation it came from, put two files side by side, delete one, or
download them all as one zip. A text file's tile shows its first lines; any text file can be read
in place (Markdown, CSV, JSON, code, logs), while HTML and SVG are only downloaded.

![The Files gallery with a picture open: who delivered it, when, its size and checksum.](/screenshots/files.webp)

On a narrow screen the list, the conversation and the panel take turns: pick an Agent to see its
conversation, and open the panel from the conversation's bar. Above the list, **Waiting for you**
names the Agents waiting on a question or a command, one tap from it.

## The address

Everything on screen is in the query string of `/`, so a link or the back button lands on the same
screen: `agent`, `team`, `panel` (`agent`, `team`, `connections`, `changes`, `files` or `none`),
`view` (`connections`, `changes`, `files`), `conversation`, `file`, `result`, `note`, and `section`
for an open sheet. [Attention](/docs/operations/#attention) items link into the workspace, for
example `/?agent=<id>&section=learning`.

## Manage

**Manage** holds the housekeeping pages under one tab bar. Each shows what there is first and
creates in a dialog from the button at the top right; the rarer and destructive actions of a row
are behind its **⋯** button, and one Bazilion would refuse is shown disabled, with the reason.

![Manage, Agents: Agents grouped by Team under a search box and a Current, Archived and All filter, each with its template and model, and a menu on each row.](/screenshots/manage-agents.webp)

- **Agents** (`/agents`): every Agent, grouped by Team. Find one by name, creature or vibe; show
  current, archived or all. Each row says its template and the model it answers with. Archive,
  restore, move to another Team, delete, and **Add an Agent**.
- **Teams** (`/teams`): **Create a Team** asks for a name and where its files are: a new folder in
  `~/.bazilion/teams/<id>/`, or a project folder it links to. A Team can be removed once it has
  no Agents; removing it removes only the link to a project folder.
- **Agent templates** (`/templates/agents`): what a new Agent starts with. A template's page has a
  **Settings** card (name, default model, skills at spawn) and a **Files** card for `SOUL.md`,
  `IDENTITY.md`, `BOOTSTRAP.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `TOOLS.md`, each card saved with one **Save**.
  Edits affect future spawns only.
- **Team templates** (`/templates/teams`): rosters, one Agent per slot from an Agent template, and
  the connections between them. Arrange them as a map or a matrix; **Spawn** creates them in a new
  Team or one that exists.
- **Skills** (`/skills`): **Import skills** from OpenClaw, a folder or a `.zip`. A risky scan
  finding or a `SKILL.md` that cannot be read is shown under the skill. Attach skills through a
  template or under **Skills** in an Agent's side panel.
- **Usage** (`/usage`): what your Agents' model calls used, today or over 7, 30 or 90 days or
  everything. The money spent on providers paid per use leads; the tokens of subscriptions (a
  ChatGPT plan, Copilot, a token plan) and of models on your computer are counted apart, at no
  cost. A bar per day, readable with the arrow keys and as a table, then the totals by Team, Agent
  and model; open a Team or an Agent for theirs only. Each conversation's use is under its title in
  the conversation menu. The cost is what the provider billed when it says, otherwise the
  catalog price, marked as an estimate. From a terminal: `bazilion usage`.

![Manage, Usage: $96.57 spent in the last 30 days, 23M tokens of which 5.8M on a subscription, a bar per day, and the totals by Team.](/screenshots/usage.webp)

## Setup (`/config`)

- **Models** (`/config`): the chat models your Agents think with, the image model they draw
  with, and the providers behind them, each with what its last test found. **Add a provider**
  connects one in a single step that tests it before anything is saved; **Add models** searches
  every connected provider ([providers and models](/docs/configuration/#providers-and-models)).
- **Tools** (`/config/tools`): web search, the page reader, the browser, how commands run and
  message chains, one card each. A card says what is in effect and is saved whole
  ([setting the tools](/docs/tools/#setting-the-tools)).
- **MCP** (`/config/mcp`): stdio and Streamable HTTP servers ([MCP](/docs/tools/#mcp-servers)).
- **Telegram** (`/config/telegram`): Telegram credentials, preflight, pairing, the allowlist
  and Attention notifications ([Telegram](/docs/telegram/)).
- **Access** (`/config/access`): device tokens and browser sessions.
- **System** (`/config/system`): the installation's health, how Agents run commands, and
  **Download backup**, which saves an archive of the home.

![Setup, Models: six chat models with their size, price and who uses them, the image model, and three providers that each say when they were last tested.](/screenshots/models.webp)

A key is shown as saved, never in full. Replacing or removing one is its own action.

## Attention (`/attention`)

What needs you. First what an Agent is waiting on right now: its question, or a command waiting for
your approval, soonest to expire first, each with the time it stops waiting; **Answer** and
**Decide** open the conversation. Then lesson proposals, failed learning reviews and triggers,
stopped message loops, queues paused after a restart, and background turns that failed: a
follow-up or a turn for new mail, one item per Agent and cause, so an expired key is one line
however many turns it stopped. Each item links to the Agent's
conversation or sheet in the workspace. The page follows changes as they happen. See
[daily operations](/docs/operations/#attention).

![Attention with one open item: Quill is waiting for your answer, with the time it stops waiting and an Answer button.](/screenshots/attention.webp)
